# RandallDavis-PCBDesignConsultation

Job Description: 
Required an experienced hardware engineer to conduct a focused design review of a custom PCB. The board is designed around the NXP i.MX RT1062 microcontroller and was developed in KiCAD v9. After receiving the manufactured boards, we are unable to establish a connection between the iMXRT1062 chip and a PC. We need an expert to review the design files to identify potential flaws that could cause this connectivity issue.
 
Scope of Work:   
1. The primary goal of this initial, short-term contract is to identify the likely cause of the connection failure.
2. Review the schematic and PCB layout in KiCAD v9.
3. Focus specifically on the iMXRT1062 and its critical support circuits, including:
       o USB interface (both physical and data lines).
       o Power delivery network (PDN), including rails, decoupling, and sequencing.
       o Clocking and reset circuits.
4. Identify any design errors, layout issues (e.g., poor signal integrity, incorrect trace impedance), or component selection problems that could prevent communication with the MCU.
 
Deliverables: Concise report outlining:
1. A list of any critical design flaws or areas of high concern.
2. Clear recommendations for design modifications or specific areas to probe on the physical PCB for further debugging.
3. An assessment of whether the issue is likely a design flaw versus a manufacturing or assembly defect. Based on your initial findings, this project may be extended for further consultation.
